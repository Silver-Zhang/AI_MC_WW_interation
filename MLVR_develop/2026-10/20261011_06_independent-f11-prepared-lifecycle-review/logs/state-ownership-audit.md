## REVIEW CONCLUSION

Task 05 covers the material state groups identified before its design was read: it neither treats `SetZero` as sufficient nor treats an H2O AA result as generic fission validation. It also preserves the Stage 1 single-loop rule. These are strengths of the **design**.

No proposed owner method exists in current production source, and no sequence has exercised the matrix. The matrix therefore establishes an implementation checklist, not a verified lifecycle guarantee.
